# Who CensusChat is for, and why it exists

This page holds the product framing that used to sit in `README.md`. It was moved here
because the README's reader is a developer deciding whether to run the project, and the
framing below is aimed at a different person.

## The problem it addresses

Healthcare strategy teams wait weeks and pay consultants for demographic analysis built
on published American Community Survey data. CensusChat answers the same class of
question directly from that data.

The consulting baseline is an estimate drawn from the maintainer's own experience buying
and delivering this work. It is not a published benchmark, and this repository does not
contain evidence for it.

## Who it is aimed at

- Business analysts at health systems and Medicare Advantage plans
- Strategy teams planning market expansion and facility placement
- Researchers doing population health work

## The kinds of question it is built around

- Medicare eligibility and age 65+ demographics for a market
- Senior care demographics for facility planning
- Population health indicators and social determinants
- Side-by-side comparison of two regions

## What you can actually get today

There is no subscription and no self-serve plan. See the status section in
[README.md](https://github.com/hollandkevint/CensusChat/blob/main/README.md) — authentication is unimplemented, there is no billing, and
there are no production users. Two things are real:

1. **A delivered analysis.** Describe the question. Kevin Holland runs it and sends back
   the Excel-ready result. Fixed price, scoped and quoted before any work starts.
2. **Early access to the self-serve product**, free while it is being built.

Both start at [kevin@kevintholland.com](mailto:kevin@kevintholland.com).
